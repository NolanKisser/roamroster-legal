RoamRoster is a travel game: you unlock real places by standing in them, share them with friends, and keep a scrapbook of trips. This policy says what that involves collecting, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and, if you sign up with one, a password (stored hashed by our hosting provider; we never see it). If you continue with Google or Apple, they share your name and email with us. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one.
  • Profile: your username, display name and emoji avatar.
  • Precise location: only as described in section 2.
  • Progress: the places you unlock and when, your XP, level, prestige, badges, challenges completed and countries visited.
  • Photo places: when you add a place from a photo, we store the photo, the place's name, country, note and coordinates, your device's reported accuracy, and details read from the photo file: when it was taken and, if present, where (its GPS tags). We also store fingerprints of the image (a hash and a perceptual hash) to spot duplicates.
  • Scrapbook: photos you add, their caption and place name, the date they were taken, the location from the photo's GPS tags or one you look up, the camera model, image fingerprints, and an automatic assessment of whether the photo's details look authentic.
  • Social: posts, likes, comments, friend requests and friendships, and the notifications they create.
  • Wishlist: places you save.
  • Safety: reports you file (what, why, and any note) and people you block.

We don't collect payment details, contacts, government ID or advertising identifiers.

2. Location, specifically

While the app is open, your phone reads your GPS position to show where you are and which places are in range. That reading stays on your device.

When you tap Unlock, your coordinates at that moment, their accuracy, and whether your device reports a simulated location are sent to our server once, so it can check you're really there. The server makes that decision, not the app. To catch impossible travel, it compares the place with the one you last unlocked and how long ago.

We don't keep a history of where you've been. From an unlock we store only the outcome: which place, and when.

Photos are different: a photo you upload may carry the GPS location where it was taken, and we keep that with the photo, as described above.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game: verifying unlocks and awarding XP, prestige and challenges.
  • To run the social features: your feed, friends, comments, notifications and leaderboards.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing submitted places and reports, and enforcing blocks.
  • To operate your account and reply when you contact us.

4. Who can see what

WhatWho can see it
Profile and progressAny signed-in user: your username, display name, avatar, level, XP, countries count, and which places you've unlocked and when. This powers search, friends and leaderboards.
Posts and likesYou and your accepted friends.
CommentsAnyone who can see the post. A person who blocked you won't see yours.
ScrapbookYou and your accepted friends.

Our moderators are people we appoint to review submitted places and reports. They can see the content under review, and the reported content with its author.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage, and processes data only on our behalf.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • Maps: on Android the map is drawn by Google Maps, and on iOS by Apple Maps. To load the map, your device requests map tiles from them directly, subject to their privacy policies.

We disclose data beyond this only if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

6. Keeping and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it: profile, progress, posts, likes, comments, friendships, scrapbook and photo-place photos, notifications, wishlist, and the reports and blocks you made. Places you added that were approved stay in the catalog, no longer linked to you. Copies may remain in our provider's routine backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.

7. Your choices

  • Delete individual posts, comments, scrapbook memories and pending photo places at any time.
  • Turn off location access in your device settings. You can still browse, but you can't unlock places.
  • Block people, and unblock them from your profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at the address below.

8. Children

RoamRoster isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules limit every account to its own private data, and the server (not the app) decides unlocks, rewards and who can see what. No system is perfectly secure, but these are the protections we'd want for our own accounts.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, such as what we collect or who sees it, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions, data requests or deletion help: support@roamroster.world.
